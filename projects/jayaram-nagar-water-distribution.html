Project overview

This SUEZ internship project covered two District Metered Areas (DMAs) in Coimbatore: Jayaram Nagar and VOC Park. Existing network data and population point data with latitude/longitude were supplied by the SUEZ team. The work included population forecasting, demand calculation, DEM-based elevation preparation in ArcGIS, WaterGEMS hydraulic modelling, review of pressure and velocity results, pipe-diameter refinement, and valve placement for sectional isolation.

Detailed working methodology

01

Define the design basis for both DMAs

The work covered Jayaram Nagar and VOC Park DMAs in Coimbatore, with a design period of 2025–2055. The population data used for the work was supplied by the SUEZ team, with 2018 used as the base-year population data.

Applicable CPHEEO design guidance was considered for the water-supply design and DMA pocket/valve arrangement.

02

Forecast the design population

Used the geometric increase method to forecast population for both Jayaram Nagar and VOC Park DMAs from the supplied population data.

The forecast population supported demand calculations over the 2025–2055 design period.

03

Estimate water demand in Excel

Calculated domestic demand using 135 litres per capita per day (LPCD) and included non-domestic demand for both DMAs.

Water losses and fire demand were also included. Demand calculations were prepared in Excel before allocation to the WaterGEMS model.

04

Prepare the WaterGEMS network model

Used existing network data provided by the SUEZ team and prepared the model, checking pipe diameter, material and roughness properties.

Junction elevation data was not supplied; elevations were independently extracted from a DEM using ArcGIS/GIS and then used in WaterGEMS. GIS was also used for data visualization.

05

Allocate demands to junctions

Used Excel demand calculations alongside population point data containing latitude and longitude. Demand was allocated to nearby network junctions using a spatial allocation workflow in WaterGEMS.

The exact LoadBuilder allocation method (for example, Nearest Node versus another spatial method) has not been independently reconfirmed, so this page does not assert a specific method.

06

Run hydraulic analysis in WaterGEMS

Run the model and review the hydraulic outputs, including flow in pipes, pressure at junctions, velocity and hydraulic grade line where relevant.

Investigate warnings or unexpected results by checking connectivity, demand assignments, elevations, pipe properties and boundary conditions. Do not treat a successful model run alone as proof that the design is acceptable.

07

Review results and refine pipe diameters

Reviewed junction pressure and pipe velocity in WaterGEMS FlexTables, checking minimum/maximum pressure and pipe velocity against applicable design criteria.

Adjusted pipe diameters based on the pressure and velocity results and design criteria, then reran the hydraulic simulation to review the updated performance.

08

Plan DMA pockets and valve isolation

Planned smaller pockets within both DMAs and placed valves so sections could be isolated independently for monitoring and leakage control.

Pocket sizing and the number of connections were considered against the applicable CPHEEO criteria. The public portfolio does not state a specific numerical pocket limit because the exact criterion has not been verified.

09

Prepare technical deliverables

Prepared technical reports, WaterGEMS model files, hydraulic result tables, network drawings/maps, demand-calculation spreadsheets, population-forecasting sheets, GIS maps and presentation slides.

Client-specific model files, internal screenshots, project data and reports remain private; this page summarizes the workflow without publishing confidential deliverables.
